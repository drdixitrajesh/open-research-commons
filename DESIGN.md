# Design

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

An academic field notebook informs the photographic cover and the editorial typography. Archive contents use a compact sans serif, visible classifications, and quiet rules.

- Navy #111e43 and paper #fbfaf7 are the large surfaces. Cobalt #263bc9 identifies actions and subject links. Coral #f9a16c marks the primary contribution action in the cover.
- Instrument Serif gives headings their character. DM Sans gives controls and records a readable rhythm. Script-aware fallbacks preserve readable multilingual layouts.
- The cover opens into a catalog search. Record pages prefer citations, languages, dates, and subject classifications over visual decoration.
- The decorative cover photo depicts a botanical field notebook and skeleton leaf. It was generated for the project.
